# Methodology

Tower Map assigns tenants to floors through an editorial scoring framework.

The goal is not to create a perfect financial ranking. The goal is to create a
readable map of ecosystem signal.

## Core Signals

### Market Gravity

Market gravity reflects relative size, liquidity, and visibility.

Signals may include:

- market capitalization
- liquidity depth
- trading presence
- exchange visibility
- ecosystem relevance

### Cultural Heat

Cultural heat measures attention and narrative force.

Signals may include:

- community activity
- meme velocity
- social visibility
- recurring mentions
- recognizable identity

### Continuity

Continuity rewards projects that remain present over time.

Signals may include:

- historical relevance
- active maintenance
- repeated usage
- long-term ecosystem role

### Native Fit

Native fit asks whether the asset behaves like a real Solana ecosystem tenant.

Tower Map generally excludes:

- stablecoins
- wrapped assets
- bridged assets
- staked SOL derivatives
- LP tokens
- tokenized stocks
- tokenized ETFs
- treasury and fund products

## Floor Logic

Higher floors represent stronger combined signal.

The exact floor is not only a market cap rank. A tenant can live higher because
it has unusual cultural gravity, infrastructure importance, or long-running
ecosystem relevance.

## Contract Addresses

Contract addresses should only be displayed after verification from an official
source or trusted registry.

Unverified addresses should remain marked as pending.

## Review Rhythm

The tower should be reviewed regularly.

Tenants can move floors as market gravity, cultural heat, liquidity, and ecosystem
role change over time.
