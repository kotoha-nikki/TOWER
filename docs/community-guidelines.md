# Community Guidelines

Tower Map is an editorial map of ecosystem signal. Tenant Notes are a small
public layer for context, memory, and civil commentary around each tenant.

## Good Notes

Good notes help visitors understand a tenant or its place in the tower.

Examples:

- Useful project context
- A correction with a source
- A thoughtful opinion about category or floor placement
- Historical context about a tenant's role in the Solana ecosystem
- A short observation that adds signal without becoming promotional noise

## Keep It Useful

Do:

- Stay on topic for the tenant profile.
- Be specific when making claims.
- Link to credible sources when asking for a correction.
- Keep criticism about projects, data, or placement, not about people.
- Use the data update issue template for registry corrections.

Do not:

- Spam links, tickers, referral codes, or repeated slogans.
- Post phishing links, wallet-drainer links, malware, or scam instructions.
- Impersonate Tower Map, tenant teams, maintainers, or wallet holders.
- Share private information or doxx anyone.
- Harass, threaten, or target people with abuse.
- Present market outcomes as guaranteed.
- Use Tenant Notes as a paid promotion surface.

## Reports

If a note looks harmful, misleading, abusive, or unrelated, open a content report
through GitHub:

```text
.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/content_report.yml
```

Include the tenant slug or profile URL, the note context, and the reason for
review. Screenshots are helpful when available.

## Moderation Actions

Tower Map may hide or delete notes that break these guidelines. Severe or
repeated abuse can lead to wallet-level restrictions.

Moderation is used to keep the public surface readable and safe. It is not used
to remove good-faith disagreement about floor placement, methodology, or tenant
relevance.
